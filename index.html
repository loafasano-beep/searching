Reading Guide

How the wall turns live attention into motion.

The wall updates from Google Trends and turns current search activity into a field of living clusters. Each cluster starts with one search, then builds outward through category, traffic, texture, and relation to nearby signals.

Source

The live feed drives cluster text, category, traffic, and source details shown on the wall.

Category

Each category defines the palette and the style of the cluster, while the search text adds unique detail.

Scale

Traffic affects cluster size and ring density, so higher activity looks larger and brighter.

Motion

Motion is subtle. Drift and rotation keep the field alive without distracting from the data.

Connections

Lines show visual proximity, not causation, and help the wall read as a shared landscape.

Navigation

Hover to inspect a cluster. Click once to lock your view on it. Press H to hide the interface for a clean gallery view, and press Esc to step back.

Focus Mode (Close-Up)

Double-click any cluster (or click a locked cluster again) to enter Focus Mode. This isolates the signal so you can inspect its individual layers, from rings to texture.

Onboarding

The walkthrough uses one live example so the visual mapping is easy to follow before the full field appears.
